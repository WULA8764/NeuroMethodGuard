# Example EEG/ERP Methods Text with Deliberate Reporting Gaps

Thirty-two undergraduate students participated in the experiment. EEG was recorded with a 64-channel Brain Products system. The EEG was sampled at 500 Hz. Data were filtered from 0.5 to 30 Hz and segmented from -200 ms to 800 ms relative to stimulus onset. Baseline correction used the -200 to 0 ms interval.

Trials with large artifacts were rejected. ICA was used to remove ocular artifacts. After preprocessing, approximately 28 trials per condition remained.

For the ERP analysis, we focused on N170, N400 and LPP. The N170 was measured at PO7 and PO8. The N400 and LPP were selected based on the largest difference in the grand average waveforms. Repeated-measures ANOVAs were conducted for each component. Significant P3/N400/LPP differences reflect semantic and emotional processing differences between conditions.
